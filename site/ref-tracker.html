Experience levels

Based on which jobs someone has reffed before. The level is the job they can ref next time.

Level 1: Deep judge
Do this a few times before moving up.
Level 2: Blitz
For anyone who has played a season. Everyone should be able to do this by the end of their first season.
Level 3: Line of scrimmage
For anyone who has played for a few years.
Level 4: Head ref
See Head ref in the cheat sheet.
