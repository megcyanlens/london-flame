    Need support now?

    You don't have to wait for anyone to ask. Talk to a captain or coach, or our safeguarding lead (see the safeguarding policy for contact details).

    If you're having a really hard time, Samaritans are there day or night: call 116 123 for free, or visit samaritans.org. In an emergency, call 999.
