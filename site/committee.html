Other possible roles

Film Chair

Set up the camera tripod and record games (and some trainings). Get videos off the camera and into Google Drive. Stitch clips together if needed. Swap batteries during gamedays if needed. Record, or delegate recording of, close-up gameplay and B-roll on gamedays.

Kit & Merch Chair

Manage Football America, Nuola and any other merchandise sites for London Flame (remove, adjust or add items). Design new items and open team votes. Coordinate bulk orders and the preseason kit order.

Team captains

Leadership on the field
Motivate, set a positive example and boost team morale during practices and games. Show and share football knowledge and training.
Communication liaison
Be the bridge between players, coaches and committee members, making sure updates and strategies are clearly shared. Be approachable and dependable, someone players can confide in and trust.
Game & team support
Help keep game days on plan and on time, guide in-game decisions when needed, and help teammates stay focused and together. Be the team's voice in communicating respectfully with referees to resolve issues or confusion in-game.

Pitch booking details

Requirements

Non-negotiable.

  • Each flag pitch is 70 × 25 yards, with 5–10 yards of space around each sideline. Two pitches fit very tightly on a 9v9 soccer/rugby field, or three on an 11v11 field. Most game days use 2–3 pitches.
  • Astroturf only. Grass is no longer allowed from the 2026–27 season.
  • No 2G astroturf (it causes injuries).
  • Scheduled on the right weekend from the schedule sheet.
  • Must start at or after 10:00 and finish by 18:00.
  • Allow 1 hour for arrival and setup before kickoff (this doesn't always need to be in the booking, depending on the pitch), then 1 hr 15 min per game. Games are 40 min plus halftime and usually run longer with penalties. 10–15 min between games lets referees submit scoresheets and players take a break to eat and use the toilets. A 4-game day means 5–6 hours.

Preferred

People will be unhappy otherwise, but we can if we have to.

Mornings
Most game days run within 10/11:00–16:00. Afternoon bookings (13:00–18:00) are allowed but mean late, inconvenient travel for other teams.
Saturdays
Most game days are Saturdays, and our team prefers them. If nothing is available, the game day can be on the Sunday of that weekend.

Pitches

Chiswick Rugby Club
Our home pitch.
Loughborough College
Far to travel, but used by many teams and likely free for us to book. The main astro field and the rugby field are both options.
Brunel University
Great location for us, with high-quality 4G turf. Usually around £80–100 per hour, with limited availability.
