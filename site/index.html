About the club

London Flame is a women's flag football club based in London, with team(s) competing in the Women's Flag Football League (WFFL) across Europe. Our mission is to develop competitive skill, build teamwork, and enhance the quality of the women's game in Great Britain.

How to join

  1. Come try us out for free. Message us on Instagram or check the club calendar to see when our next training is, then meet us at Clapham Common.
  2. Join the club. When you're ready, pay the membership fee, then request to join the WhatsApp community: London Flame Community on WhatsApp.
  3. Want to play in the WFFL? Let us know after you join the club and we'll assess whether you're a good fit for one of our teams.

Club fees

First training is free. After that, training and season participation require membership.

Club membership (if approved) £15 Per season. Goes towards equipment (balls, playbooks) and club expenses. Pay club membership

WFFL team fees (if selected)

Team membership £15 Per season. Contributes to home game pitch booking for your team and the BAFA team registration fee. Pay team + club membership (includes the club fee).
Kit top £30 / £33.50 Required top: £30 sleeveless or £33.50 with sleeves. Shorts not required. See Kit.
WFFL membership & insurance £40 Bought from BAFA's JustGo site, bafa.azolve.com. Register for London Smoke club, then the London Flame or Blaze team. Follow the JustGo signup guide.

Also needed: boots for the WFFL season, with plastic studs (not metal). Flags and gloves recommended. Shorts must have no pockets, or zipped pockets.

Tournaments

  • All tournaments are at-will and open to all club and/or team members.
  • We'll give plenty of notice and details for each tournament, and prioritise the events members want to attend.
  • The tournament's team registration fee is split among attending players.
  • Travel and accommodation are managed by individuals or coordinated by player groups.
  • You may play for other teams at any tournament. We expect you won't choose another team over Flame if both attend the same event, and that you won't share playbook, strategy or any competitive intelligence.

Pickup for flag football tournaments: Discord server.
